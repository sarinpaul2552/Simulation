# NexLearn V3 — Release summary

NexLearn V3 gives facilitators three market worlds, more credible economics and executive decisions whose costs students can see. V2 stays available unchanged, for comparison with earlier cohorts and for every existing session.

## For facilitators

- **Choose a market path when you create a session.** All teams face the same market, and the path cannot be changed once play starts.

  | Path | Main pressure |
  |---|---|
  | AI Acceleration | AI hype against willingness to pay |
  | Enterprise Transformation | Delivery capability and capacity |
  | Economic Pressure | Liquidity and resilience |

- **The path is shown:**
  - on your dashboard;
  - on the projector, with no codes or PINs;
  - in every board report.

  Students never see its name during play.
- **Everything from Batch 10A and 10E works per path:**
  - Class Discussion, timelines, the final debrief and the board report;
  - the Q3 signal panel and its gate: assessments appear only after every team finishes Q4, in the path's own wording.

## For students

- **Q5 contract.** The offer differs by path ($48M, $64M or $32M a year). Accepting uses part of the $30M budget for implementation, and the screen says how much.
- **Q6 recession stance.** Every team declares one:
  - **Protect** — keep investing;
  - **Slow** — keep at least $5M as cash;
  - **Cut** — take cost actions.

  A hiring freeze moves People money to cash automatically.
- **Financed expansion.** The CFO can add up to $10M a quarter to the budget (Q2–Q7) with newly raised money. The money costs interest or dilution and must leave $25M of cash.
- **Ceiling warning.** A warning appears when a capability is close to what the organization can use, and the results show any investment wasted at a ceiling.

## Economics (V3 engine, `v3-econ-1.0`; valuation NXLN-VAL-1.1)

- **AI** pays according to the adoption it supports, not as a perpetual flow. AI-only falls from 8th to 11th in path A and drops further where AI demand is weaker.
- **Enterprise** capability converts into wins and revenue within the game: +63% Enterprise revenue for Enterprise-only, and in path B Enterprise + AI is second only to the evidence-reading adaptive team.
- **University** partnerships produce visible revenue (about $20M by Q8) without instant jumps.
- **Organizational health matters:**
  - overload erodes culture;
  - culture recovers when the pressure eases;
  - low culture strains delivery;
  - investors price organizational health.

  Deep layoffs still raise profit, but lower the share price for most strategies.
