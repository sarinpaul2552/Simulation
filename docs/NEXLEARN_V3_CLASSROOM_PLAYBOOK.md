# NexLearn V3 — Classroom playbook (90 minutes)

This playbook extends `docs/BATCH10E_FACILITATOR_GUIDE.md`, which remains the reference for the facilitator screens. Only what V3 adds is described here.

**The timings are design targets; they have not been validated in a timed rehearsal** (see `BATCH10F_CLASSROOM_TIMING_REPORT.md`).

## Before class

1. **Choose the market path.** It cannot be changed once a team starts.

   | Path | Use it to teach |
   |---|---|
   | A · AI Acceleration | Reading AI hype against willingness to pay; defending a disrupted core |
   | B · Enterprise Transformation | Building delivery capability ahead of demand; contract scale against capacity; workforce constraints |
   | C · Economic Pressure | Liquidity, cost structure, workforce decisions and resilience in a deep downturn |
   | Original NexLearn (V2) | Comparability with earlier cohorts |

2. Create the session: Facilitator → New session.
   - Set class mode (MBA or Undergraduate) and commitment mode (team commitment recommended).
   - Choose the market path, then the teams.
3. Keep the admin PIN private. Use the **Projector view** on the room screen: it hides PINs and team codes and labels teams anonymously.
4. Students never see the path's name or theme. They see its story, quarter by quarter.

## Running the session

| Time | Step | V3-specific notes |
|---:|---|---|
| 0–5 | Opening | Teams join with their codes. Five roles per team (shared device or one device per player). |
| 5–23 | Q1–Q3 (6 min each; stage clock "Fast quarter") | Q3: each team cites one or two signals. The evidence options are the same in every path; the evidence itself differs. |
| 23–32 | Q4 (9 min; "Major quarter") | Strategic destination. Paths reward different destinations: Enterprise in B; Consumer defence in C. |
| 32–34 | Q4 signal review | Each team sees "Looking back: which signals mattered?" for its path. In Class Discussion, the Q3 panel opens only when every team has finished Q4. Path C's lesson is the reverse of path A's: usage *understated* the paying loss. |
| 34–70 | Q5–Q8 (9 min each) | Q5: the contract uses part of the $30M budget. Ask "what did you not fund to make room?" Q6: every team declares Protect, Slow or Cut. Ask why, and what they refused to cut. CFO: a team may borrow or issue shares to invest up to $10M more in Q2–Q7. Ask what it costs later. |
| 70–90 | Final debrief (20 min) | Final debrief tab, the board report and defence questions built from each team's own recorded reasoning. |

## Debrief prompts that use V3

- **Contract:** "It cost $X of your budget. Which capability did you give up for it, and was it worth it in this market?"
- **Stance:**
  - "Protect kept building; Cut kept cash and profit; Slow kept options. Which did your market reward?"
  - In the laboratory no stance won on every measure, so the answer depends on the team's position.
- **Expansion:** "Did the extra investment land in capabilities that could still grow? The warning tells you when they cannot."
- **Organization:** "Your plan ran at X% of capacity. What did that do to culture, and what did culture do to delivery?"
- **Market path (only after the game):** reveal the path's theme. Ask which signals pointed to it, and when.

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Cause / action |
|---|---|
| "NexLearn V3 market paths are not available on this database yet" | The V3 migration has not been applied. Use Original NexLearn, or apply the migration (deployment plan). |
| A team cannot submit in Q6 | No stance chosen; "Slow" needs at least $5M kept as cash ("Move $X to cash"). Cost actions need the "Cut" stance. |
| A team cannot submit in Q5 after accepting | Keep the contract cost as cash ("Reduce every investment in proportion to make room"). |
| Expansion refused | It must be covered by new money raised in the same quarter (net of repayments), at most $10M, Q2–Q7, keep $25M cash, and not with "Slow". |
