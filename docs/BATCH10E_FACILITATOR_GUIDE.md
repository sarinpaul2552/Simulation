# NexLearn facilitator run sheet (Batch 10E)

**For:** a 90-minute session. All times are estimates (see `BATCH10A_CLASSROOM_TIMING_PLAN.md`).

## Before class

- Create the session.
- Choose **facilitator-led** pacing and **individual devices** if the room allows.
- Keep the PIN private. Projector view hides the PIN, team codes and team names.

## Screens

| Tab | Use it for | When |
|---|---|---|
| **Live progress** | Who is where; each team's last committed priority; open the next quarter | Throughout |
| **Stage clock** (under the tabs) | A countdown: 6:00 for fast quarters, 9:00 for major quarters, 20:00 for the debrief. Guidance only: it never opens, advances or locks a quarter, and students do not see it on their devices. | Each quarter |
| **Class discussion** | One quarter, all teams: **intended → decided → happened → learned**; the class pattern line; financial comparison | After Q2, Q4 and any quarter you want to discuss (about 1–2 min) |
| **Team timelines** | One team across quarters: continuity, adaptation and potential misalignment (described, not scored) | Before the final debrief, or to prepare questions for a team |
| **Final debrief** | Running order; where teams ended; biggest changes of direction; Q3 evidence → Q4 commitment; "what would you do differently?" | The last 20 minutes |
| Leadership and awards | Existing scorecards and awards (unchanged) | Optional |

## Suggested moments

1. **After the Q2 results.** In Class discussion → Q2, read the stance line ("Stances: 2 × Accelerate · 1 × Maintain…"). Ask two teams with different stances to defend them.
2. **After the Q3 results.** In Class discussion → Q3, see which signals each team cited. **Do not reveal the assessments.** They stay hidden until every participating team has completed Q4, and there is no override.
3. **After the Q4 results.** The Q3 panel now shows the assessments. Ask: "Which of your cited signals held up? What will you trust next time?"
4. **Final 20 minutes** (Final debrief tab):
   - 5 min: teams read their board report. It now includes "Intent and outcome" and questions quoting their own reasoning.
   - 8 min: board defence. Each team defends one decision; sample teams if there are more than four.
   - 7 min: class debrief. Biggest changes of direction, Q3 → Q4, and "what would you do differently?"

## Projecting safely

- **Projector view** shows teams as *Team A, Team B, …*, hides team codes and the PIN, and shows **no written reflections** unless you select them.
- To show a reflection, use normal view: open **Review written reflections**, read the notes, and tick the ones to display. Then switch to projector view. Only the ticked notes appear, with anonymised labels.
- Structured learning choices (for example "We would make a different call now") appear as counts and are safe to project.

## How to read the indicators

- **Change of direction:** an investment area moved by **$5M or 15 percentage points** of the budget. When the budget changed (Q8 options) or a decision capped investment, only shares of the money invested are compared, and the context is stated.
- **Continuity / adaptation:** descriptions, not judgements. Ask what the team saw that made it change, or stay.
- **Potential misalignment:** a stated priority with a clear money signature did not match the allocation by a stated rule (listed under "How directions are described"). It is a question, not a verdict.
