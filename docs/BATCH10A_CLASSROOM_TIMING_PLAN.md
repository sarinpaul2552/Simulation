# Batch 10A — classroom timing plan (target: 90 minutes)

> **ESTIMATES ONLY — NOT MEASURED.** Every minute figure below is a planning estimate. None comes from a stopwatch or a classroom. The only measured facts are tap counts, taken from the code. A stopwatch pilot (`BATCH10_V3_VALIDATION_PLAN.md` part E) must confirm or replace these figures before the 90-minute claim is used with clients.

## 1. Session plan: 90 minutes, of which 20 for the final board report and debrief

| Segment | Minutes | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Opening: company, roles, rules | 5 | Facilitator-led. Teams press "Begin" together. |
| Q1–Q3: fast quarters | 18 | 3 × 6 |
| Q4: major quarter | 9 | The destination commitment |
| Q4 signal reveal ("Looking back: which signals mattered?") | 2 | Teams skim it in Results & Learn. The full discussion moves to the final debrief. |
| Q5–Q8: major quarters | 36 | 4 × 9 |
| **Final board report and debrief** | **20** | See section 3 |
| **Total** | **90** | |

The decision screen shows the matching hint as text: "Fast quarter · about 6 minutes for the whole quarter" (Q1–Q3) and "Major decision · about 9 minutes for the whole quarter" (Q4–Q8). A unit test keeps those hints consistent with this plan (3 × 6 + 5 × 9 = 63 minutes of quarters). No timer is enforced in 10A.

## 2. Inside a quarter (estimates)

| Stage | Fast quarter (Q1–Q3) | Major quarter (Q4–Q8) |
|---|---|---|
| Understand: private briefs, read in parallel on individual devices | 1.5 | 2 |
| Discuss & Decide: allocation, event card, rationale | 3 | 4.5 |
| Commit | 0.5 | 0.5 |
| Results & Learn: Level 1 summary, one reflection choice | 1 | 2 |
| **Quarter** | **6** | **9** |

**Batch 10A's effect on these times (estimates):**
- **Rationale:** 4 taps per quarter (measured: priority, evidence, confidence, one risk), 6 in Q3. That is the same number as before 10A. The added thinking is targeted at 45–75 seconds and fits inside Discuss & Decide.
- **Prefill:** removes up to five typed amounts each quarter (an estimated 20–60 s saved when used).
- **Reflection:** one required choice and an optional line. It is saved as students go, so a refresh costs no re-entry.
- **Deep analysis:** stays folded. In a 90-minute session, teams should not open it during quarters.

## 3. Final board report and debrief: 20 minutes

| Part | Minutes | Content |
|---|---|---|
| Teams read their board report | 5 | Final report, verdict and defence prompts |
| Board defence | 8 | Each team defends one decision. About 2 minutes each for 4 teams; with more teams, cut to 1 minute or sample teams. |
| Facilitator debrief | 7 | Cross-team comparison; the Q3 signals (which evidence held up and which did not); "Knowing what you know now, what would you do differently?" |

## 4. Conditions for 90 minutes

Each condition below is an assumption, not a measurement.

1. **Individual devices.** Briefs are read in parallel. A shared team device adds an estimated 1–1.5 minutes per quarter for the brief hand-off, about 10–15 minutes over eight quarters (≈100–105 minutes in total). Use individual devices for a 90-minute slot, and keep the shared device for longer slots.
2. **Facilitator-led pacing.** The facilitator opens each quarter on time. Self-paced teams drift.
3. **The time box is kept.** The facilitator calls time at 6 and 9 minutes. A visible timer is planned for 10E. Until then, the facilitator uses a clock.
4. **No deep analysis during quarters,** and reflections stay at one choice plus an optional line.
5. **Class size about 4–6 teams.** The board defence needs sampling above 4 teams.

**If a session runs late**, protect the 20-minute debrief by:
- shortening Q6 and Q7 to 8 minutes each (+2 minutes);
- skipping the Q4 reveal skim, since it is covered in the debrief (+2 minutes);
- sampling 3 teams for the board defence (+2 minutes).

## 5. What the pilot must measure

1. Time per stage and per quarter, Q1–Q3 against Q4–Q8, on individual and shared devices.
2. Time spent on the rationale picks alone (target ≤ 75 s).
3. How often prefill is used, and how much of the allocation is changed afterwards.
4. Whether the 20-minute debrief is protected: actual start and end times.
5. Total session time.
