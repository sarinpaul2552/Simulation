# Batch 10A — classroom timing plan

> **ESTIMATES ONLY — NOT MEASURED.** No figure in this document comes from a stopwatch or a classroom. Every figure is a planning estimate that extends the Batch 10 timing model (`BATCH10_EDUCATIONAL_DESIGN_REVIEW.md` §6). A stopwatch pilot (`BATCH10_V3_VALIDATION_PLAN.md` part E) must confirm or replace them. The only *measured* facts are tap counts, taken from the code.

## 1. What Batch 10A changes for time

| Change | Effect on time (estimate) | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| One risk instead of two | −5 to −15 s per quarter | One fewer tap and less debate over a second risk. |
| Strategic priority (new, one tap) | +10 to +30 s per quarter | One tap; the discussion it prompts is the point. |
| Q3 evidence pick (one or two taps) | +20 to +45 s in Q3 only | Teams name their sources aloud. |
| Prefill from last quarter (optional) | −20 to −60 s per quarter when used | Replaces typing up to five amounts; adjustments still take time. |
| Reflection saved on change | 0 s; avoids re-entry after a refresh | Previously a refresh lost the reflection. |
| Accountability panel and signal reveal | +0 to +60 s per quarter; the reveal +3 to +5 min once, at Q4 | Reading time. The reveal is the facilitator's natural debrief moment. |

**Taps for the rationale (measured from the code):** 4 per quarter before Batch 10A (belief, confidence, two risks); 4 after (priority, belief, confidence, one risk); 6 in Q3 (plus one or two signals). The added rationale stays within the 45–75 seconds per quarter target set in the brief. This is an estimate.

## 2. Quarter pacing (shown to students as a text hint)

| Quarters | Pace | Hint shown on the decision screen | Suggested quarter length (estimate) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Q1–Q3 | Fast | "Fast quarter · suggested about 8 minutes" | 8–10 min including briefs, commit and results |
| Q4–Q8 | Major | "Major decision · suggested about 12 minutes" | 11–14 min |

The hint is text only. No timer is enforced: the facilitator timer is deferred to Batch 10E.

## 3. Session plan (estimates)

| Segment | Individual devices (recommended) | Shared team device |
|---|---|---|
| Opening (company, roles, rules) | 10 | 10 |
| Q1–Q3 (3 × fast) | 24–30 | 30–36 |
| Q4 including the signal reveal | 14–17 | 16–19 |
| Q5–Q8 (4 × major) | 44–52 | 52–60 |
| Final report and debrief | 12–15 | 12–15 |
| **Total** | **≈104–124 min** | **≈120–140 min** |

**Reading.** Batch 10A alone is not expected to reach a 90-minute session. It moves the estimate down by about 5–10 minutes from the Batch 10 model (individual devices ≈115–135), mainly through the single risk and the prefill, while adding the reveal. Reaching about 90–100 minutes still depends on:
- the facilitator timer and stage control (10E);
- individual devices as the default;
- the facilitator using the fast/major rhythm.

These are estimates.

## 4. Supporting both device modes

- **Individual devices.** Every player sees the decision screen; the rationale picks are team-level and saved to the shared draft. Only the CEO commits (unchanged). Reflections are saved on change; if two players edit at once, the last save wins.
- **Shared device.** Unchanged hand-off for briefs. The rationale is entered once by the team at the shared screen.

## 5. Facilitator guidance (no new screens)

- Q1–Q3: hold teams to about 8 minutes. Ask each team to read out its priority or stance before committing.
- Q3: ask each team which one or two signals it cited, and why. Do not comment on which signals were right; the answer comes after Q4.
- After the Q4 results: give 3–5 minutes for "Looking back: which signals mattered?". Ask: "Which of your cited signals held up? What will you trust next time?"
- At every reflection: "Knowing what you know now, what would you do differently?"

## 6. What the pilot must measure

1. The time from opening the decision screen to "Review and commit", per quarter, Q1–Q3 against Q4–Q8.
2. The time spent on the rationale picks alone (target ≤ 75 s).
3. How often prefill is used, and how much of the allocation is changed after it.
4. Reveal reading time at Q4, and whether teams refer to it in later reflections.
5. The total session time by device mode.
