# Q3-Q8 Revised Economics Table
## Explicit Strategic Decisions, Path Dependency, Multidimensional Scoring

---

## OVERVIEW: Key Revisions

1. **Q4 Destination is Explicit Decision** (not inferred from allocation)
   - New decision screen: Teams explicitly choose Consumer / Enterprise / AI-native / Balanced
   - Q1-Q3 allocation/capabilities determine "readiness" for execution
   - Path dependency: Teams cannot choose AI-native if AI capability < 35 (low readiness)

2. **Balanced Has Strategic Ceiling**
   - Reduces volatility (lower downside, but also lower upside)
   - Terminal revenue ceiling ~20% below coherent strategy
   - Should be "safer but smaller," not "safe AND winning"

3. **Q5-Q8 Test Different Dimensions**
   - Q5: Market validation (does strategy have demand?)
   - Q6: Competitive moat (can you defend position?)
   - Q7: Organizational execution (can team actually scale?)
   - Q8: Terminal outcome (multidimensional scorecard)

4. **Path Dependency Throughout**
   - Q5 validation success depends on Q4 capability readiness
   - Q6 competitive defense depends on invested capabilities
   - Q7 execution depends on culture/talent Q1-Q3 decisions
   - Q8 final score blends financial + capability + organizational dimensions

5. **Terminal Score is Multidimensional**
   - Financial performance: Revenue, EBITDA, cash (33%)
   - Strategic position: Market share, capability leadership, defensibility (33%)
   - Organizational health: Culture, talent retention, execution alignment (34%)
   - Overall "winning" depends on balance, not pure revenue
